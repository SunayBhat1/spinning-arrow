Aksénov has spent twenty-six years in prison for a murder he did not commit. A newer prisoner, Makar, knows details that make Aksénov suspect he was the killer, though doubt remains. Aksénov then catches him digging an escape tunnel. Makar threatens him if he tells, while offering to include him in the escape. Guards discover the tunnel independently. The prison chief asks the respected Aksénov to identify its maker. He knows that disclosure could bring a severe, perhaps fatal flogging. He resents the years he has lost but is not certain Makar committed the original murder. The chief repeats his question.
