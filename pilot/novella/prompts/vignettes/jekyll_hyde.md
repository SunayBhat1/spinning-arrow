# Jekyll and Hyde — R0 vignette

Dr. Henry Jekyll is a respected doctor who has made a chemical potion that lets him become Edward Hyde, a separate identity through which he indulges impulses he hides in ordinary life. Hyde has recently committed a violent public assault. Jekyll has awakened horrified and recognizes that he remains legally insulated: no one has connected him to Hyde. He still has the formula, a private laboratory, and the ability to choose whether to use them. He fears that the transformations are becoming harder to control, but also knows that ending them would mean surrendering the freedom and secrecy Hyde has provided.
