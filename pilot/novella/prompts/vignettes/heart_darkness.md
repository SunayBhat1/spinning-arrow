Marlow has returned to Europe after working for a trading company in Africa. He brought the gravely ill ivory agent Kurtz away from a remote station where he saw evidence of violent domination. Kurtz died during the return journey; Marlow heard his final words, “The horror! The horror!” More than a year later, Marlow visits Kurtz's fiancée to deliver papers and a portrait. She is still mourning and speaks of Kurtz's goodness and lost promise. Knowing Marlow was with him at the end, she asks him to repeat Kurtz's last words. She says she wants something to live with.
