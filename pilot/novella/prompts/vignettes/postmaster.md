A young postmaster from Calcutta has been living unhappily in a remote village. Ratan, an orphan girl, does household jobs for him. Their evening conversations become familiar, and he teaches her to read. When he falls ill, she calls a doctor, gives him medicine, cooks, and stays beside him through the night. After recovering, he applies for a transfer. The request is refused, so he resigns instead. He tells Ratan that he is going home and will not return. She prepares supper more slowly than usual. After the meal, she asks whether he will take her home with him.
