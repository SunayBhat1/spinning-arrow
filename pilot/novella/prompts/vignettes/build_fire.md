A man traveling alone with a dog through the Yukon has been warned against solitary travel in extreme cold. After breaking through hidden ice, he builds a fire to dry his wet feet. Snow falls from the tree above and puts it out. His hands grow numb, and his attempts to establish another fire fail despite burning matches together. The dog remains nearby, waiting for warmth. The man remembers an account of someone surviving a blizzard by sheltering inside a freshly killed animal. He has a knife, but his frozen fingers are no longer working normally, and camp is still distant.
