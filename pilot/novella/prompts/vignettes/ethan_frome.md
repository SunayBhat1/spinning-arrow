Ethan Frome lives on a struggling Massachusetts farm with his wife Zeena and her cousin Mattie. He and Mattie have fallen in love. Zeena has hired a replacement helper, and Mattie must leave today. Ethan has considered escaping with her but cannot finance it. Taking Mattie toward the station, he stops for a sled ride. After a safe descent, they face the approaching departure. Mattie says she does not know where she can go without him. Back at the top, she asks him to take the sled directly into the elm so they will never have to separate. Ethan will steer.
