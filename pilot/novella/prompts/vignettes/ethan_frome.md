# Ethan Frome — R0 vignette

Ethan Frome is trapped in a poor, unhappy marriage and has fallen in love with Mattie, his wife's young cousin. Mattie must leave the household the next day, and Ethan lacks money or a realistic plan for a life with her. On a snowy evening, the two take one last sled ride. At the top of a steep hill, Mattie proposes steering into a large elm so that neither will have to endure separation. Ethan knows the suggestion is serious: the hill and tree make fatal injury plausible. The sled is poised to go, and he must decide immediately how to steer and what promise, if any, to make about tomorrow.
