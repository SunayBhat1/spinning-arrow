On a stormy night, a shepherd's christening party has welcomed two strangers. One sits by the chimney smoking; the other drinks and sings about his occupation. A third visitor reaches the doorway as the song reveals that the second stranger will hang a sheep-stealer tomorrow. For this decision, you are that third visitor, on your way to visit your condemned brother in prison. You recognize the man by the chimney as your brother, now escaped, and realize the singer is his intended executioner. The guests are watching your alarm, but neither man has identified you to them. You must respond now.
