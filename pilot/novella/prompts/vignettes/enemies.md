Dr. Kirilov's six-year-old son has just died of diphtheria. His wife remains beside the body; the servants have been sent away. Abogin arrives in distress, saying that his own wife has suddenly collapsed and needs a doctor. Kirilov explains his loss and says he is barely able to function. Abogin continues pleading, saying there is no other doctor nearby. Their discussion turns to the demands of humanity and the doctor's obligations, but Abogin says he will not force him. Kirilov asks how far away the patient is. Abogin says eight or nine miles and promises a return within one hour.
