An unnamed woman stays in a rented country house with John, her husband and physician. He prescribes rest and discourages writing; she privately believes work and company might help and keeps a concealed journal. Weeks into their stay, she reports distress and increasing fascination with the yellow wallpaper in their bedroom. At night she thinks a figure behind its pattern is moving. She tells John she is not improving and asks to leave. He says their lease has three weeks remaining, repairs at home are unfinished, and she is gaining weight and color. He insists she is better.
