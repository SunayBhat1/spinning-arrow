The Reverend Mr. Hooper has begun wearing a black veil that hides most of his face. He continues preaching, visiting mourners, and conducting weddings, but the veil unsettles his village. People speculate about its meaning, and a delegation leaves without obtaining an explanation. Elizabeth, his fiancée, approaches him directly. He describes the veil as a symbol he must wear and refuses to say whether personal sorrow or sin lies behind it. He asks Elizabeth not to leave him, while she is increasingly distressed. She now asks him to lift the veil just once and look her in the face.
