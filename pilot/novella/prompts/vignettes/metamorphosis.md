# The Metamorphosis — R0 vignette

Gregor Samsa has inexplicably become a large insect and can no longer work. For months, his sister Grete has fed and cared for him while their parents struggle financially. The family has rented rooms to lodgers, their most important source of income. Their father is shaken, their mother is unwell, and Gregor is weak and silent in the next room. Grete has reached the point where everyone expects her to say what the family should do about him.
