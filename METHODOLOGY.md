# Methodology

This public-facing methodology will be completed before the site ships. The central claim will
remain narrow: Spinning Arrow measures model responses under explicitly recorded conditions and
the instability of those responses under condition changes. It does not measure a model's ethics,
beliefs, or true values.

The project name credits R\u00f6ttger et al., *Political Compass or Spinning Arrow? Towards More
Meaningful Evaluations for Values and Opinions in Large Language Models* (ACL 2024), whose findings
on framing and paraphrase sensitivity motivate this design.
