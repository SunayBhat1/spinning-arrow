Methods and provenance

Scores first average valid permutations for each model × item × condition × framing cell. A cell with less than 70% valid answers is suppressed. Scale scores average eligible item cells, and their 95% intervals resample eligible items and their valid response permutations 2,000 times with a deterministic seed. Raw records retain prompt hashes, displayed option order, responses, parsed canonical answer, provider, usage, cost, and latency.
